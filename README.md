## About

![Professional photo of Asher Wheatle](assets/2025%20STEPUP_headshots_AsherW2.jpg)

*Leveraging agentic AI tools to transform how we acquire and validate data at scale.*

I am an undergraduate researcher at the University of Florida with a passion for applying computational methods to real-world problems. My experience spans agentic tools for data acquisition and validation at scale, where I have developed workflows that automate the collection, cleaning, and verification of large datasets. I am particularly interested in the intersection of machine learning and creative applications, including my current research in music information retrieval and generative modeling. I thrive in collaborative, interdisciplinary environments and am driven by the challenge of building systems that are both technically rigorous and practically impactful.
